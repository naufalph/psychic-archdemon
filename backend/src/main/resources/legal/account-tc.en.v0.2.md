## 3. About Rumantra

Rumantra is a digital platform that connects Service Users with Service Providers through search, project submission, bidding, communication, verification, and project administration facilitation.

In its early stage, Rumantra focuses on architecture and design services. Rumantra may expand, restrict, or change the categories of services available on the platform from time to time.

Unless otherwise stated in writing, Rumantra acts as a platform provider, marketplace/bidding intermediary, communication facilitator, contract facilitator, and payment/escrow facilitator. Rumantra is not a party that directly provides architecture, design, construction, technical supervision, or other professional services.

Rumantra does not absolutely guarantee the quality, feasibility, timeliness, legality, or suitability of a Service Provider's work, except as expressly stated in a contract or specific document that binds Rumantra.

## 4. Account Types

### 4.1. Service User Account

A Service User Account may be created by individual homeowners, small developers, SMEs, companies, contractors seeking architects or design providers, or other parties requiring services through Rumantra.

### 4.2. Service Provider Account

A Service Provider Account may be created by parties providing architecture, building design, interior design, space planning, design visualization, or other related design services approved by Rumantra.
